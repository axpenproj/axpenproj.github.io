motion graphics that land on the beat

Fifteen animated templates — kinetic type, morphing shapes, glitches, data counters — that you fill with your own words, photos and videos. Every cut and every hit is timed to a soundtrack the app writes for your edit.

Tap any text in the preview to change it, pick from eighteen typefaces, swap backgrounds, then export an MP4 up to 2K straight to your gallery.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, and no internet connection — the app does not even ask for one.
