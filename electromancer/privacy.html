Privacy Policy — Electromancer

Last updated 2 October 2026

Electromancer is a game published by Axpenproj. There are no accounts, no sign-in and nothing to buy, and your progress stays on your device. The Android version shows ads from Google, and the section on ads below says exactly what Google receives. Axpenproj receives nothing at all.

The short version

There are no accounts, no sign-in and no way to contact other players. Your progress stays on your device and is never uploaded. There is no server, no analytics, no crash reporting and nothing to buy. The Android version shows ads supplied by Google, which is the only thing in the game that uses the network; the game itself works with the device offline, without ads.

What is stored on your device

The furthest round you have reached in each storm, whether you have finished a run, the record of which creatures you have met and how many of each you have felled, which of the game's lines you have already been told, your sound setting, and how many rewarded ads you have watched today, so the game can stop offering them past its daily limit. All of it is written to the game's own private settings storage on the device. It never leaves the device, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

What leaves your device

Only what the ad provider needs, described below. The game sends nothing of its own anywhere: no analytics, no crash reports, no account, and your progress never leaves the device. Axpenproj receives nothing at all.

Ads

The Android version shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, for a second chance after a defeat or for extra charge during a round, and an occasional full-screen ad before a round begins. To serve and measure them, Google may collect your advertising ID (a resettable identifier), your approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information such as model, OS version, app version and language, and ad interactions such as impressions, clicks and completed rewarded videos. This processing is carried out by Google, not by Axpenproj, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices about ads

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before requesting personalised ads, and PRIVACY CHOICES in the Settings menu reopens that form so you can change your answer at any time. Anywhere in the world you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, under Google then Ads on most Android devices. Rewarded ads are never required to play, and the game limits how many it offers in a day.

Permissions

The game asks for no runtime permissions. It does not request your location, your contacts, your storage, your camera or your microphone. The ad SDK uses the internet connection and may read your advertising ID, as described above.

Children

Electromancer is not directed at children under 13 and Axpenproj does not knowingly collect personal information from anybody, of any age. It has no chat, no player-to-player features and no user accounts.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is collected by Axpenproj, there is nothing held by Axpenproj to delete. To remove what is stored on your device, uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings. Either erases your progress permanently, and there is no copy of it to restore from. What Google holds for advertising is controlled from your device's ad settings and from your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and a materially different version will also be reflected in the game's store listing. The version you are reading is the one shipped with the build you are playing.
