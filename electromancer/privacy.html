Privacy Policy — Electromancer

Last updated 14 September 2026

Electromancer is a game published by Axpenproj. There are no accounts and no sign-in, it works entirely offline, your progress stays on your device, and nothing you do in the game is sent to Axpenproj or to anybody else.

The short version

There are no accounts, no sign-in and no way to contact other players. Your progress stays on your device and is never uploaded. There is no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and nothing to buy. The game makes no network requests of its own and works with the device offline.

What is stored on your device

The furthest round you have reached in each storm, whether you have finished a run, the record of which creatures you have met and how many of each you have felled, which of the game's lines you have already been told, and your sound setting. All of it is written to the game's own private settings storage on the device. It never leaves the device, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

What leaves your device

Nothing. This build collects no identifiers, contains no advertising or analytics code, and has no way to send anything anywhere. Axpenproj receives nothing at all, which also means Axpenproj has no idea who you are or that you are playing.

Permissions

The game asks for no permissions beyond what it needs to draw and make noise. It does not request your location, your contacts, your storage, your camera or your microphone, and it does not read your advertising ID.

Children

Electromancer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anybody, of any age. It has no chat, no player-to-player features and no user accounts.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is collected by Axpenproj, there is nothing held anywhere to delete. To remove what is stored on your device, uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings. Either erases your progress permanently, and there is no copy of it to restore from.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and a materially different version will also be reflected in the game's store listing. The version you are reading is the one shipped with the build you are playing.
