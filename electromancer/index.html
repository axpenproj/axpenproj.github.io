ELECTRO
MANCER

walking is what charges the storm

A top-down arena where the one button you have is paid for by the only other input you have. The overcharge meter fills from the distance you actually walk, so standing still to fight is the same as choosing not to.

Every hit leaves a charge behind, and a charged enemy that dies arcs into whatever is standing next to it. Twenty rounds, an upgrade after every one, and all twelve weapons reachable in a single run.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, and no connection required.
