Privacy Policy — Stack Chaos

Last updated 6 August 2026

Stack Chaos is a game published by Axpenproj. There are no accounts and no sign-in, your progress stays on your device, and nothing you do in the game is sent to Axpenproj.

The short version

There are no accounts, no sign-in and no way to contact other players. Your progress stays on your device and is never uploaded. There is no server, no analytics and no crash reporting, and this build shows no ads at all.

What is stored on your device

Levels cleared, star ratings, best times, best Endless depth, your coin balance, which cosmetic items you own and which you have equipped, and your sound setting. All of it is written to a single file in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

Nothing. This build makes no network requests of its own, collects no identifiers, and has no way to send anything anywhere. It works entirely offline.

Children

Stack Chaos is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It has no age-gated content and no player-to-player features.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is collected by Axpenproj, there is nothing held to delete. To remove what is stored on your device, uninstall the app or clear the app's data in your device settings. Both erase your progress permanently.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and a materially different version will also be reflected in the app's store listing.
