Police Chase
Gate Runner

Your speed is the health bar.

Get it on Google Play Play on web Screenshots

Four lanes of night road, two of them oncoming traffic, and police behind you in waves. Your speed never stops draining, so every row of gates is the same decision: one good lane out of four.

A cruiser that reaches you does not end the run. It telegraphs the lane it is moving into, which you can then dodge. Free to play on Android, or in your browser on Playgama.
