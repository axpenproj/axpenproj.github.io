The short version

The game itself collects nothing and has no accounts and no purchases. The Android version shows ads from Google, and the section on ads below says exactly what Google receives. Axpenproj receives nothing at all.

What is stored on your device

One save file, written by the game and readable only by it. It holds your upgrade levels, your bank, your best distance and best time, how many runs you have played, and your control and effects settings. Nothing in it identifies you.

What leaves your device

Only what the ad provider needs, described below. The game sends nothing of its own anywhere: no analytics, no crash reports, no account, and your save never leaves the device. It does not read your location, contacts, files or accounts, and it asks for no runtime permissions.

Ads

The Android version shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. To serve and measure them, Google may collect your advertising ID (a resettable identifier), your approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information such as model, OS version, app version and language, and ad interactions such as impressions, clicks and completed rewarded videos. This processing is carried out by Google, not by Axpenproj, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices about ads

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before requesting personalised ads, and PRIVACY CHOICES in the Settings menu reopens that form so you can change your answer at any time. Anywhere in the world you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, under Google then Ads on most Android devices.

Playing on the web

If you play the game on a website, that website may show its own ads under its own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing there either.

Purchases

There are none. Nothing in the game can be bought.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and Axpenproj does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes the save file and with it everything the game has ever stored. Axpenproj holds no copy anywhere else, because the game never sent one. What Google holds for advertising is controlled from your device's ad settings and from your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this document changes with it. Last updated 22 September 2026.
