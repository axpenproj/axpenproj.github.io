The short version

This game does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no accounts and no purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What is stored on your device

One save file, written by the game and readable only by it. It holds your upgrade levels, your bank, your best distance and best time, how many runs you have played, and your control and effects settings. Nothing in it identifies you.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The game makes no network requests. It does not read your advertising ID, your location, your contacts, your files or your accounts, and it asks for no runtime permissions.

Ads and purchases

There are none. The game is not monetised, shows no advertising, and contains no in-app purchases.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes the save file and with it everything the game has ever stored. There is no copy anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.
